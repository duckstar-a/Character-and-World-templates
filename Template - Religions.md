# Beliefs
**Creation Story:** (how does this religion explain how the world/universe was created? What about their deity (if applicable)? What about the species and people that follow it? What about other things?)
**Destruction Story:** (how does the universe end, according to this religion? Is it a violent, destructive one? Is it a quiet, peaceful one? Is it cyclic?)
**Rulebook/s:** (does it have any? How many? What are they called? Is it required to follow them?)
**Rituals:** (what rituals are performed during regular prayers? What are rituals for marriage, childbirth, getting new property? Before starting a job or a new school? For travel? What about a competition or a stage performance? What about mealtimes?)
**Induction:** (how are new followers inducted into the religion? Do they have to perform a ritual? Or can they just decide that they're That religion now and learn and practise on their own? What are the views on this?)
**Other:** (does the religion focus on individuality or community? What kind of activities does it promote? If a believer violates any of its rules, what is done to them?)
